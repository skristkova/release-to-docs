# Ringo 4.12

## New: AI call summary
Recorded calls now get an automatic AI summary (key points, next steps, customer sentiment). Admins turn it on in **Settings → Recording → AI call summary**. Available on the Pro and Enterprise plans. Summaries appear on the call detail page next to the recording.

## Changed: Reports is now Analytics
The **Reports** section in the left menu is renamed **Analytics**. All existing dashboards moved with it. The **Export to CSV** button moved from the top right to the **⋯** menu on each dashboard.

## Changed: Recording retention
Default recording retention goes from 90 to 180 days on the Pro plan.
