# Record calls

Ringo can record inbound and outbound calls so you can review them later or use them for training.

## Turn on call recording

1. Go to **Settings → Recording**.
2. Switch on **Record calls**.
3. Choose who is recorded: all users, or selected teams.
4. Click **Save**.

## Listen to a recording

1. Open **Calls** in the left menu.
2. Click the call you want.
3. Press **Play** on the call detail page.

## How long recordings are kept

| Plan | Retention |
|---|---|
| Starter | 30 days |
| Pro | 90 days |
| Enterprise | Custom |
